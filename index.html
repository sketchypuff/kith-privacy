Kith Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026. Applies to Kith for iPhone and Kith for Android.

In short: Kith keeps everything on your phone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no Kith servers. The developer never sees your people or anything you write about them.

What Kith stores

The people you add and what you set up for them: their name, how often you want to catch up, when you last did, important dates such as birthdays, tags, notes, and the time zone you pick for them. Kith also remembers your settings, such as your default reminder time.

All of this is stored on your device. None of it is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Your contacts

Kith asks for access to your contacts so you can add someone from your address book. It reads the card you choose to fill in their name and birthday. Kith shows the contact's photo and uses their phone number for the Call, Message and WhatsApp buttons, but reads both straight from your contacts each time and never keeps a copy. Kith does not upload your address book or read cards you haven't added.

You can say no. You can still add people, though Kith won't be able to show their photos or fill in their birthday.

Sync and backup

iPhone: if iCloud sync is on in Kith's settings, your people sync between your own devices through your private iCloud account. Apple handles that storage under Apple's privacy policy. The developer can't see it. You can turn sync off at any time.

Android: Kith has no sync and no access to the internet. If you have turned on backup in your phone's settings, Android may include Kith's data in your own Google account backup, handled under Google's privacy policy.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled and delivered by your phone itself. Nothing passes through a server.

Privacy lock

If you turn on the lock, Kith asks your phone to check that it's you using Face ID, a fingerprint or your passcode. Kith only learns whether the check succeeded. It never sees your face, fingerprint or passcode.

What Kith doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete someone from the People list and they and everything about them are removed. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. On iPhone, data synced to iCloud can be removed in your iPhone's Settings under your name, then iCloud.

Children

Kith is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect anything from them. In any case, it collects nothing from anyone.

Changes
